There are 3 styles.
text1:
 	Used for the main body of the HTML page.
	Headings in this area will use the bold tag to distinguish from regular text and links.
	font-family: Verdana, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif
	font-size: x-small

burgundy :
	Used for the Left Menu area on Templates with a left area.
	Headings in this area will use the bold tag to distinguish from regular text and links.
	font-family: Verdana, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif
	font-size: x-small
	color: 560808

silverbox :
	Used for any special text you wish to place a border around. Works well with paragraphs.
	This is a silver area with a black border around it, with black colored text the same size as text1.
	font-family: Verdana, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif;
	color: #660000;
	font-size: x-small;
	border: 1px solid #000000;
	background-color: #eeeeee;
	margin: 4px;
	padding: 4px;
	width: 100%;
